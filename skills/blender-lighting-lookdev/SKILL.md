# Blender Lighting & Look-Dev

Physically plausible lighting, time-of-day atmosphere, and architectural camera look-dev.

## Architectural Lighting Recipes

| Time of Day | Sky / Environment | Key & Practical Lights | Mood & Contrast |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Siang (Daylight)** | Clear sky + high Sun (elevation 45–60°), soft white cloud fill | Crisp directional sun shadows; practical lights off or subtle indoor fill | Fresh, high visibility of natural brick, stone, wood, and foliage |
| **Sore (Golden Hour)** | Warm low Sun (elevation 12–25°, azimuth 45° off-axis), warm peach/amber horizon | Long dramatic shadows + warm 2700K practical LED strips & globe lamps turning on | Inviting, warm golden highlights on corrugated white container & red brick |
| **Senja / Malam (Twilight Blue Hour)** | Deep twilight blue sky (`#0b1324` to `#1c2b48`) | Warm 2700K–3000K under-counter bar LEDs, backlit shelves, frosted white glass globe post lamps, underwater pond light, brick wall uplights, and stair step LEDs | High-contrast cinematic warmth against cool blue ambient sky |
