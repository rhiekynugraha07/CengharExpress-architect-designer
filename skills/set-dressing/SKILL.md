# Set Dressing (Interior & Exterior Spatial Furnishing)

Story through placement. Functional seating clusters. Clear circulation paths.

## Zone Density & Clearance Rules

| Zone | Furnishing Strategy | Clearance Requirement |
|------|---------------------|-----------------------|
| **Order & Barista Bar** | Espresso machine, 2 grinders, POS, 3–4 bar stools under awning, backlit shelves | ≥ 1.8m queuing & walk-by space in front |
| **Centerpiece Walkway** | Timber deck + reflecting water pond with natural boulders | ≥ 1.2m clear walk path on each side |
| **Communal Zone** | Long cast-concrete table (3.0–4.0m) with integrated shade tree cutout & 6–8 stools | ≥ 0.9m pull-out clearance behind stools |
| **Courtyard 2–4 Pax** | Teak folding chairs & tables on 1×1m concrete stepping slabs over gravel | ≥ 1.2m center-to-center table spacing |
| **Terraced Amphitheater** | Built-in dark concrete bench tiers with seat cushions & side drink tables | Unobstructed side staircase (≥ 1.5m wide) |
